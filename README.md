## *About SwiftBite*
#### Swiftbite website is a platform for ordering premium sweets and delicacy.It is built to cater to Le Petit Desserts Business needs.

#### Le Petit Dessert was established in 2026 by six people for their ENT 300 project. This business sells chocojar and other desserts such as pudding and cheesecake. The people that were in this project were Nana Tajudin, Laila Ismail, Alexa Tan, Kiar Rahman, Lara Majnun and Sam Lim.

#### Based on the organizational chart in 1.2, Nana Tajudin is in charge of  being the  project manager. She oversees the project progress and makes important decisions. While Laila Ismail is in charge of creative works such as designing posters and marketing for the project.

#### Next, Alexa Tan is in charge of financial recordings and analysis. Other than that, Kiar Rahman was assigned to be in charge of documenting the business and doing all the paperwork.

#### Lastly, Lara Majnun and Sam Lim are responsible for logistics and making sure that the correct order arrives on time.
